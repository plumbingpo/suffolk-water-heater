Kitchen Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temperature dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater working alongside the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, with service scheduled around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a critical issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can result in carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, Legionella growth in water stored too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Suffolk Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

After-Hours Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout Suffolk and the surrounding area, and scheduled work can be done overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

Our first priority is pinpointing the fault and getting hot water back fast, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Preventive Care

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Suffolk Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.

Compared with an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and failed inspections. Properties that stay on a schedule usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Call us to talk through a plan sized to your property and budget.
