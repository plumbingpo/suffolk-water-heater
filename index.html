Water Heater Replacement in Suffolk

If the tank itself is leaking, repair is off the table, and a replacement typically runs $1,100 to $2,800 installed for a standard tank or $2,800 to $5,500 for tankless. If water is leaking from the tank body, the hot water runs rusty, or a tank unit is past 10 to 12 years old, replacement is the smarter investment, because a corroded tank cannot be patched. Our technicians walk you through both numbers honestly so you can decide with real information.
